What is disdroc

The chat you know — on your terms.

Everything you'd expect — servers, channels, DMs, voice and video — but it lives on a machine we run ourselves, not someone else's cloud.

Runs on our hardware

We operate the chat service on hardware we own. Your group just opens disdroc.com in a browser.

Your messages aren't for sale

We never sell your data, and ads never appear inside your conversations. The privacy page lists what is stored and every outside provider we use.

Free, and here's why

No paid tiers, no premium, no "boosts." The developer pays for the hardware and internet; optional ads in shared activities help cover it.

Features

Everything a community needs

The full chat stack — plus a couple of things most chat apps don't have, like an AI that runs on the server itself.

01

Servers & channels

Text and voice channels, categories, direct messages and group chats — the structure you already know your way around.

02

Voice & video

Hop into a call with screen sharing on desktop. Calls connect reliably, even on locked-down home and mobile networks.

03

Local AI assistant

Ask a question or draft a message with a model running on disdroc's own hardware (llama.cpp), without an external AI API. Prompts and replies are stored as chat messages.

04

Custom roles & permissions

Create as many roles as you need and pick exactly what each can do — or keep it simple with a single admin role. Your call.

05

Privacy & safety

Trusted devices that won't sign you out when you travel, email two-factor codes, an 18+ gate and click-to-reveal blur on explicit media.

06

Everywhere you are

Use it in any browser, install the native Android app with real incoming-call notifications, or grab the desktop app for Windows and Linux.

Why it matters

Before moving your conversations, know where they go. We store messages and files on hardware we operate. Private chats can be end-to-end encrypted, per chat and only if you turn it on. Community channels are not, and the website uses outside providers. Read the details before you join.

Questions

Good to know

disdroc is a real, working chat app made by one developer, nadirisim. It is not a company and has nothing to sell you. You can check it before signing up: the client code is on GitHub, the status page shows uptime, and the release notes list every update by date. We will never ask for your password, a card or a crypto payment outside disdroc.com. If someone does, it isn't us.

Yes. There are no paid tiers, no premium and no "boosts", and we never ask for card details. disdroc runs on hardware the developer already owns, so the monthly cost is mostly electricity and internet. Optional ads in shared activities help pay for it; they never appear inside your messages.

Messages and files are stored on hardware operated by disdroc. Private chats can be end-to-end encrypted, per chat and only if you turn it on. Community channels are not, and metadata stays visible to the service. Cloudflare and optional media providers also process data; our privacy and trust pages explain the details.

Calls use WebRTC: they connect directly when possible and use disdroc's own TURN relay when a direct connection is unavailable. Some networks may still block calls. Try a call with a friend and check Support if it cannot connect.

Disdroc AI runs on the operator's hardware without an external AI API. Your prompts and replies are stored as chat messages by the service, with the same privacy limits as other text conversations.

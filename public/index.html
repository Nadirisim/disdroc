What is disdroc

The chat you know — on your terms.

Everything you'd expect — servers, channels, DMs, voice and video — but it lives on a machine we run ourselves, not someone else's cloud.

1

Runs on our hardware

Messages, files and calls live on a box at home — not a rented cloud. Open disdroc.com and you're on it.

2

No ads, no tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, nothing sold to anyone. The software is the product — not you.

3

Free forever

No paid tiers, no premium gates, no "boosts." Every feature, for everyone, at no cost.

Features

Everything a community needs

The full chat stack — plus a couple of things most chat apps don't have, like an AI that runs on the server itself.

01

Servers & channels

Text and voice channels, categories, direct messages and group chats — the structure you already know your way around.

02

Voice & video

Hop into a call with screen sharing on desktop. Calls connect reliably, even on locked-down home and mobile networks.

03

Local AI assistant

A model runs on disdroc's own hardware (llama.cpp). Summarize a channel, draft a message, ask a question — your prompts never leave the server.

04

Custom roles & permissions

Create as many roles as you need and pick exactly what each can do — or keep it simple with a single admin role. Your call.

05

Privacy & safety

Trusted devices that won't sign you out when you travel, email two-factor codes, an 18+ gate and click-to-reveal blur on explicit media.

06

Everywhere you are

Use it in any browser, install the native Android app with real incoming-call notifications, or grab the desktop app for Windows and Linux.

Why it matters

Your conversations belong to you. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to a server farm you'll never see. disdroc keeps every message, call and file on hardware we run ourselves — and the AI runs there too, so nothing ever has to leave.

Install notes

Windows: run disdroc-installer.exe. Use Check for updates in the app to look for a newer version.

Android: open the APK and allow installs from your browser when prompted. Play Protect may warn about an unknown developer — that's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

Linux: chmod +x disdroc-linux.AppImage, then run it.
